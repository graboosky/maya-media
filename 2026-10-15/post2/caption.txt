3 Muay Thai stance mistakes every beginner makes 🥊 (fix these before your next class)

1. Chin up → tuck it and look through your eyebrows
2. Hands drifting low → fists at eyebrow height, elbows in
3. Tightrope feet → hip-width and staggered, so you don't tip over

Quick stance check before every round: chin down, fists at your eyebrows, elbows in, feet hip-width, knees soft.
