20-minute Muay Thai workout at home 🏠 no bag, no partner (save this)

Round 1 · 3 min: jab–cross + footwork
Rest 1 min
Round 2 · 3 min: add knees (guard up, hips through)
Rest 1 min
Round 3 · 3 min: slow body kicks, balance first
Core · 3 min: plank, 30 s on / 15 s off
Cool-down · 3 min: hamstrings & hips
