I got a headache after every evening class 💧 (for 3 weeks, and it wasn't the punches)

I blamed the lights. The music. Getting hit.

Then my coach asked: "How much water did you drink today?"

2 coffees and a sip at lunch. At my desk I forgot water existed. So in warm-up I'd chug a whole bottle and slosh through round 1.

His fix: water all day, not all at once. A 1-liter bottle on my desk, empty by 5 PM. Small sips between rounds, no more chugging.

3 weeks later: no headache. Round 3 felt different too.

The headache didn't start in class. It started at my desk.

This is just what worked for me, not medical advice. If headaches keep coming back, check with a doctor.
