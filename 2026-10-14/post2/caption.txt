How I structure a 3-minute bag round 🥊 beginners: steal this timer

Minute 1: clean tech only (jab–cross, hands back to face)
Minute 2: add kicks & knees
Minute 3: power + footwork (still protect your chin)
Rest 1 min: breathe, reset stance, plan the next round

Biggest mistake I made: dumping all my gas in the first 20 seconds. Pace the round like a fighter.
