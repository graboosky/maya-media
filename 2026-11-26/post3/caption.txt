The quote on my mirror wasn't Aristotle's 🥊 (sound on, I read it every morning for 2 months)

"We are what we repeatedly do." I read it every single morning and felt very wise. Then I trained whenever. 2 classes one week, zero the next.

One night I quoted it to my coach. He smiled: "Nice. What did you repeat this week?" I had no answer.

So I looked it up. Plot twist: Aristotle never wrote it. Will Durant did, in The Story of Philosophy (1926), summing up Aristotle's ideas.

I laughed, then wrote a new note under it: Monday, Wednesday, Friday. 6 PM. 6 weeks later I haven't missed one.

The quote never changed me. The repeating did. (That last line is my own words.)
