I had no idea what the numbers meant 🥊 (sound on, for 2 months)

My coach would call "2, 3, 2" and I'd just guess. Usually wrong. A cross when he wanted a hook, and my glove hit the edge of the pad.

He lowered the pads: "Three is a hook, Maya. Not a guess."

Everyone else just knew. I was too embarrassed to ask.

So at home I set FightNome to call my punches as pad numbers. 1 jab, 2 cross, 3 lead hook, 4 rear hook, 5 lead uppercut, 6 rear uppercut. A real recorded voice calls each one on the beat, phone in my pocket. I just listen and throw.

2 weeks later he called "2, 3, 2" and my hands went before I even thought. He grinned: "Look who speaks numbers now."
